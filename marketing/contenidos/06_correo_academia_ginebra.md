**Subject:** Suggestion for the Human Rights Platform tracking tools directory: ATLAS ONU

Dear Geneva Academy team,

We are Sukima-Labs, an independent, non-profit initiative. We have built ATLAS ONU, a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0; content CC BY 4.0) Spanish-language atlas of the UN human rights system: {{mandatos}} special-procedure mandates with the resolutions that created and renewed them (checked against the UN Digital Library), {{paises}} country profiles (treaty ratification, UPR, concluding observations, visits), treaties, a glossary and a timeline. Every data point links to the official source, and inferred equivalences are labelled.

We noticed that your directory lists tools such as the Universal Human Rights Index and we believe ATLAS ONU could complement them for Spanish-speaking students and practitioners. If it fits your criteria, we would be grateful if you considered listing it under "Databases".

- Web: https://sukimalabs.com/atlasonu/
- Code and licences: https://github.com/sukima-labs/atlasonu

It is an independent project and not an official UN product. Thank you for your time.
